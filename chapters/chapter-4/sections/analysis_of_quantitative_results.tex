\section{Analysis of Quantitative Results}

This section analyzes the quantitative trends in Table~\ref{tab:quantitative_comparison_1024}. The comparison focuses on how FeatureSAGE performs against AGE and SAGE across the 102 Flowers and Animal Faces datasets at 1024~\(\times\)~1024 resolution using FID and LPIPS.

\subsection{102 Flowers}

On the 102 Flowers dataset, FeatureSAGE obtains an FID of 45.60, corresponding to a 15.8\% improvement over SAGE (54.17) and a 25.9\% improvement over AGE (61.57). This FID reduction indicates stronger distributional alignment with real flower images in the Inception feature space. For perceptual variation, FeatureSAGE achieves an LPIPS of 0.4209, compared with 0.4528 for SAGE and 0.4108 for AGE. This represents a 7.0\% decrease relative to SAGE and a 2.5\% increase relative to AGE. The LPIPS values across the three methods range from 0.4108 to 0.4528, showing that perceptual variation remains within a narrow range while FeatureSAGE achieves the best FID.

\subsection{Animal Faces}

On the Animal Faces dataset, FeatureSAGE achieves an FID of 49.60, outperforming SAGE (60.92) by 18.6\% and AGE (62.13) by 20.2\%. This reduction indicates improved distributional alignment with the real animal-face data. For LPIPS, FeatureSAGE obtains 0.4811, compared with 0.4867 for SAGE and 0.5033 for AGE. This is a 1.2\% decrease relative to SAGE and a 4.4\% decrease relative to AGE. The LPIPS values remain close across the three methods, ranging from 0.4811 to 0.5033, which indicates that FeatureSAGE improves FID while maintaining comparable perceptual variation.
