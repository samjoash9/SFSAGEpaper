\section{Objectives of the Study}

The objective of this study is to develop and evaluate FeatureSAGE, a few-shot image generation framework that improves upon SAGE by integrating the StyleFeatureEditor inverter and Feature Editor.

\textbf{Specifically, this study aims to:}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\alph*.]
    \item Integrate the StyleFeatureEditor inverter and Feature Editor into the SAGE framework to improve generated-image quality through stronger reconstruction and feature-level editing.

    \item Evaluate the generated outputs on the Animal Faces and 102 Flowers datasets using FID and LPIPS.
    
    \item Benchmark FeatureSAGE against AGE and SAGE to examine improvements in edit stability, category or identity consistency, and overall image quality.
\end{enumerate}
