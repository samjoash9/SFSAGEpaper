\chapter{Review of Related Literature}

This chapter reviews the concepts and prior studies that support the development of FeatureSAGE. It begins with the foundations of generative adversarial networks and proceeds through StyleGAN, latent-space editing, GAN inversion, and few-shot image generation to position the proposed framework within existing research.
