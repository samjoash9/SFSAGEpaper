\section{Limitations}

While the FeatureSAGE framework demonstrates effective attribute group editing in few-shot image generation, several limitations observed during experimentation warrant further research. First, the framework’s performance is highly dependent on the diversity of the training dataset, as unseen classes during validation or inference lead to errors. Second, its reliance on a pretrained StyleGAN2 generator restricts applicability to domains where such models exist, and biases in the generator may propagate to the outputs. Third, the integration of the StyleFeatureEditor and Feature Editor increases computational overhead, which may hinder scalability and real-time applications. Future research can address these limitations by exploring alternative generative architectures beyond StyleGAN2, implementing multi-scale attribute modeling to improve robustness, optimizing computational efficiency for large-scale or real-time use, and extending few-shot editing capabilities to generalize to entirely novel classes.
