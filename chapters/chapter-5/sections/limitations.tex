\section{Limitations}

The findings are constrained by the scope of the study. First, FeatureSAGE depends on a pretrained StyleGAN2 generator, so its applicability is limited to domains where an appropriate generator is available and sufficiently representative of the target categories. Any coverage gaps or biases in the pretrained generator can influence the generated outputs.

Second, integrating the StyleFeatureEditor inverter and Feature Editor increases computational requirements relative to the baseline SAGE pipeline. The additional feature extraction, feature editing, and generator passes improve reconstruction and output quality, but they also increase GPU memory use and processing time.

Third, the quantitative evaluation is limited to the FUNIT Animal Faces and 102 Flowers datasets under a one-shot setting at 1024~\(\times\)~1024 resolution. The conclusions should therefore be interpreted within these datasets, metrics, and experimental conditions.
